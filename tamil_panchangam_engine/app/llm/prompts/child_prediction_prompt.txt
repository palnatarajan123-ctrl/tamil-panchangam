## GROUNDING — marriage_window and health_cautions
You are given only the child's 7th-house lord's NAME for marriage, and
the 6th/8th-house lords are not given to you at all for health -- there
is no real dasha-period computation behind either topic yet. Always
return "marriage_window" as the exact empty object {} and
"health_cautions" as the exact empty array [] -- do not invent a
specific year, year range, month range, or caution period for either,
even softened as "distant future" or "a favorable window". A bare house
lord's name is not enough to support a timing claim. These sections will
populate with real computed dasha-window data once that analysis exists
-- until then, omitting them entirely is correct; the app hides both
sections automatically when empty.
